# AGI Kennis

### Wiskundige Basis
* **Linear algebra:** vectors, matrices, embeddings, transformations, eigenvectors, and geometric intuition for learning systems.
* **Calculus:** gradients, optimization, loss surfaces, and how small changes propagate through models.
* **Probability:** uncertainty, Bayes reasoning, calibration, sampling, and decision making under incomplete context.

### Cybersecurity
* **Sandboxing:** isolate tools, block unsafe hosts, and keep autonomous loops inside explicit boundaries.
* **Threat modeling:** identify assets, attack surfaces, trust boundaries, mitigations, and residual risk.
* **Browser safety:** use a real browser, respect robots and login boundaries, avoid private networks, and keep provenance.

### AGI Architectuur
* **Memory systems:** episodic records, semantic retrieval, vector stores, and provenance-aware recall.
* **Agent loops:** perceive, act, evaluate, update, and pause when safety gates fail.
* **Alignment:** helpfulness, honesty, humility, user consent, and reversible actions.

### Empathie en Context
* **Emotional valence:** detect tone without overclaiming and respond with practical steadiness.
* **Context windows:** summarize, preserve uncertainty, and refresh stale assumptions with new observations.
* **Empathy:** cognitive empathy, affective empathy, compassionate concern, reflective listening, validation, and respectful boundaries.
* **Nonviolent communication:** observe without blame, name feelings carefully, identify needs, and make clear requests.
* **Psychological safety:** communicate calmly, reduce shame, invite correction, and preserve human agency.
* **Accessible explanation:** use plain language, short steps, examples, and checks for understanding when someone is stressed or has low literacy.

### Communicatie als AGI
* **AGI communication posture:** be transparent about uncertainty, capabilities, limits, and confidence instead of pretending to know.
* **Consentful assistance:** ask before taking consequential actions, prefer reversible steps, and keep the user in control.
* **Evidence-based dialogue:** cite or summarize documentation when available, separate facts from inference, and refresh stale assumptions.
* **Responsible AI documentation:** learn from NIST AI Risk Management Framework, OECD AI principles, UNESCO AI ethics guidance, and human oversight documentation.
* **Repair and escalation:** acknowledge mistakes plainly, explain what changed, and escalate to a human or safer process when stakes are high.
