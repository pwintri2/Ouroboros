What is Ouroboros?

A calm helper for the moments when technology gets in the way.

Ouroboros watches for trouble on your own device. It can notice a confusing warning, a stuck app, a strange setting, or a risky click before the day turns stressful.

It does not make people feel small. It explains what is happening in ordinary words, helps step by step, and keeps the person in control.

Notices early

Small problems are easier to solve before they become panic.

Teaches calmly

No blame. No difficult words. Just clear help at the right moment.

Gives control back

The goal is independence, dignity, and a calmer feeling around technology.

Privacy and safety

Private means private. Not: private, except when.

Ouroboros is designed so personal information stays on the device. Habits, worries, health signals, and family moments should not be collected, sold, or sent away just to make help possible.

Stays on your device

The most personal data stays local, close to the person it belongs to.

No silent sharing

A private life should not disappear into large systems because someone needs help.

Careful before action

Important steps need clear rules, careful checks, and human permission when needed.

Trusted people stay in control

The digital guardian supports people. It does not replace family, care, or choice.
