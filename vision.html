What it is

A calm helper before technology becomes too much.

Ouroboros is a private digital guardian. It helps when a phone, computer, or app confuses, scares, or stops someone.

Why it matters

Most technology acts as if people already know what to do.

Real life is not like that. People get tired, worried, forgetful, or afraid to click the wrong thing. Good help should never make anyone feel foolish.

Too many steps

A simple task can become a maze when every screen asks for something different.

Fear of mistakes

People stop trying when one wrong click feels dangerous.

Quiet shame

Many people do not ask for help because they think they should already understand.

The better way

Helpful technology should feel like a steady hand.

Ouroboros watches for stressful moments, explains what is happening, and helps only as much as needed. The goal is dignity, not dependency.
