Local-First AI is an approach to artificial intelligence that prioritizes the processing and analysis of data within the edge device or on-premise infrastructure, rather than sending all data to the cloud for processing. This approach is designed to reduce latency, improve data privacy, and increase the overall efficiency of AI-powered applications. By leveraging local computing resources, Local-First AI enables real-time insights and decision-making, making it particularly useful for applications that require low-latency processing, such as autonomous vehicles, robotics, and smart homes.
