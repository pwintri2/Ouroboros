Anatomy of a proactive fix

The printer ticket that never happens.

  1. Fault

    Printer driver faults mid-spool.

  2. Perception

    Ouroboros detects the OS-level driver error instantly.

  3. Sandbox test

    A new driver configuration is tested inside a secure local container.

  4. Silent deploy

    The fix is pushed to the host OS.

  5. Resolution

    A non-technical notification appears: I noticed your printer disconnected. I have reconnected it for you.

  6. Outcome

    Zero tickets generated. Zero minutes lost.
