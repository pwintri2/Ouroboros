# Sponsorship Strategy for Ouroboros

Ouroboros (WintripAI) is an open, practical AI initiative focused on making intelligent automation more useful, transparent, and accessible — especially for elderly and vulnerable people. Sponsorship helps cover development time, infrastructure, testing, documentation, demos, and the continued public availability of the project.

## Suggested Sponsorship Tiers

| Tier | Suggested monthly contribution | Practical benefits |
|---|---:|---|
| **Community Backer** | €10–€25 | Name listed in public supporter updates when desired; early access to progress notes and demos; direct support for accessible AI research. |
| **Professional Supporter** | €50–€150 | All Community benefits, plus quarterly roadmap briefings, prioritized feedback consideration, and visibility in sponsor acknowledgements. |
| **Applied AI Partner** | €250–€750 | All Professional benefits, plus a semi-annual demo session, input on practical workflow use cases, and transparent reporting on funded development milestones. |
| **Corporate or Institutional Partner** | €1,000+ | All Applied AI benefits, plus tailored stakeholder briefings, collaborative exploration of responsible AI-agent pilots, and prominent acknowledgement on project materials where appropriate. |

Benefits are designed to preserve the independence and transparency of the project. Sponsorship does not buy control over the roadmap, access to private user data, or preferential handling that would compromise responsible development.
