Scenarios

Three practical Guardian scenarios.

The value is clearest when the interface becomes quiet and the support arrives as a simple, human message.

Scenario 1

Digital safety

A suspicious virus alert appears, audio jumps to full volume, and panic rises. Ouroboros recognizes the pattern, stops the process, lowers the audio, and reassures the user.

Scenario 2

Navigation and routine

When geographic or digital behavior deviates from a familiar route, the user does not first have to type that they are lost. Trusted guidance back to safety can activate.

Scenario 3

Human reassurance

The technical action can be complex under the hood. For the person, the message must remain simple: it is solved, you are safe, you do not have to do anything.

The proactive cycle

Intervene before panic becomes the interface.

The core loop is deliberately simple: perceive, act, evaluate, and update. Autonomy only matters when it is paired with strict safety checks.

1

Perceive

Local signals and irregularities are detected.

2

Act

The immediate risk is reduced or blocked.

3

Evaluate

The system checks assumptions, side effects, and safety.

4

Update

The state is consolidated so the next moment starts wiser.
