The shift

From transactional browser AI to an ambient operating system.

Current AI

  • Reactive: waits for a prompt.
  • Stateless: depends on fragile context buffers.
  • Isolated: trapped in browser or cloud interfaces.

Ouroboros AI

  • Proactive: works quietly in the background.
  • Contextual: understands routines over time.
  • Local-first: acts where risk actually emerges.
