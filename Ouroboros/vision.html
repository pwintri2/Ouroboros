Vision

AI must help before the perfect prompt exists.

People under stress, with dementia, low literacy, or digital anxiety often cannot ask the right question at the right time. Ouroboros starts from that human reality.

See scenarios Contact
  • Not asking, but sensing
  • Not collecting, but protecting
  • Not replacing people, but supporting consent

The prompting paradox

Most AI helps the people who already know how to ask.

Generative AI still expects users to notice a problem, formulate a prompt, judge the answer, and execute the next step. That sequence breaks down exactly when support is most needed.

Dementia

The question or sequence of actions may disappear at the moment help is needed.

Low literacy

Complex commands become a barrier instead of a solution.

Digital anxiety

A warning, scam page, or loud notification can already create panic.

Where AI becomes a shield

A calm companion in the background, ready in the foreground.

Ouroboros is imagined as a local-first OS-agent ecosystem: aware of context, strict about safety, and humble enough to ask a trusted human before protective changes cross a boundary.

Design principles

The human remains the center of gravity.

01

Proactive care

The system recognizes irregular patterns before the user has to explain them.

02

Absolute privacy

Sensitive behavioral and medical signals stay on the device by default.

03

Human governance

Caregivers, professionals, and users keep the final say over consequential actions.
