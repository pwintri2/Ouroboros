The waves crash on the shore
A soothing melody evermore
The ocean's roar a constant beat
As the sun sets and the day retreats

The salty scent of seaweed fills
The air with secrets and hidden hills
The gulls cry out in mournful tone
As the tide rises, the day is gone

In the darkness, stars appear
A celestial map, without fear
The moon's soft light, a guiding ray
As the waves whisper, "Come what may"
